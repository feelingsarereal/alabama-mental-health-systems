P: In Fiscal Year (FY) 2021, with a foundational investment and support of Governor Ivey and the Legislature, five community mental health centers (CMHC) were funded to initiate rural crisis programs and mobile crisis teams:
LI: Cahaba Center for Mental Health – Dallas, Perry and Wilcox Counties
LI: CarePath Behavioral Health – Escambia County
LI: Northwest Alabama Mental Health Center – Marion and Winston Counties
LI: WellStone – Cullman County
LI: West Alabama Mental Health Center – Marengo County
P: Two additional CMHCs were awarded funds through FY 21 federal ARPA and Block Grant awards:
LI: South Central Mental Health Center – Covington County
LI: SpectraCare Health – Houston County
P: Three Crisis Centers received state funding for six (6) Teams:
LI: AltaPointe Health – Mobile County
LI: Carastar Health (in partnership with East Central and East Alabama CMHCs) – Autauga, Elmore, Lowndes, Montgomery; Bullock, Macon, Pike; Chambers, Lee, Russell, Tallapoosa Counties
LI: WellStone - Cullman County
P: Three CMHCs received state funding for Five Child and Adolescent Teams, and are in partnership with the Alabama Department of Human Resources:
LI: AltaPointe Health – Baldwin and Mobile Counties
LI: Jefferson, Blount, St. Clair Mental Authority (JBS) - Jefferson County
LI: WellStone - Cullman and Madison Counties
