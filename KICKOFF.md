The Cowork project **📚 Manuscript Interrogator** is Product Owner. This is backlog item **K7** (`../../notes/Backlog/`, `app: alabama-mental-health-systems`).

**What this is.** A public, searchable, interactive edition of the *Alabama Mental Health Systems Report* by Little Orange Fish (Huntsville, Alabama): the October 2026 draft after every claim in it was checked against its cited source. The site has five jobs, and the author asked for each of them:

1. **Read the report**, with the source of every sentence one hover away: a summary of the source and the passage that supports the sentence.
2. **See what the check changed** and what it could not settle: a change log of 744 entries and 95 open items.
3. **Explore Alabama on a map**: 67 counties, 36 data layers, and the Department of Mental Health's own service areas, every value with its source and year.
4. **Explore the system as a graph**: the report's eight domains and four cross-sector sections, the thirteen dependencies it draws between them, and the Alabama figures behind each.
5. **Compare Alabama with the other states** on a US map built from the same kinds of data.

It runs under `mode: topic` (the report is `review.md`), with **schema extensions beyond CONTENT-PACK v0.9**, all under `content-pack/` and all specified normatively in §4b–4g below: `claims-ledger.yaml`, `changes.yaml`, `open-items.yaml`, `verification-stats.json`, `geo/`, `systems/indicators.json`, `brand/`, and block-id comments in `review.md`. `docs/` was not edited.

## 1. Hard rules

- **Never invent content.** Every sentence, definition, summary, number and label a reader sees comes from `content-pack/`. A gap renders as an amber `TODO(author)` marker (`.bx-todo`) and lists on `/methods`. This applies with full force to the maps: **no value, rank, average, total or colour class may be shown that is not either a cell of the pack's CSVs or computed from them by a rule stated in §4d**, and anything computed is labelled as computed.
- **Body text is rendered as written.** `review.md` is Little Orange Fish's report as revised on 3 October 2026. Structure it, link it, render its tables. Never rewrite, shorten or "fix" it. If you believe a sentence is wrong, put it in your scrum note.
- **Uncited prose fails the build** (APP-SPEC §6.1): any block of 25 words or more in `review.md` with no `[n]` and no `<!-- framing -->` marker is a content-build error. Do not repair one by adding a citation.
- **`<!-- synthesis -->` blocks stay visible** (there are 96: the report's own analysis, chiefly its "Gaps and opportunities"). Render each with the quiet left rule and a label that reads **"analysis"** in this app (the report calls them its "analytical reading"), give each an id, and list them on `/methods`.
- **A citation is only as good as its ledger entry.** The hover in §4b shows what the check found, not what a reader would like to see. If a ledger entry has no source for a reference number that appears in the sentence, show the reference summary alone; never synthesise a "supporting passage".
- **A broken claim anchor fails loudly.** Every ledger entry with `anchor_ok: true` must be found in its block by the rule in §4b. Zero or multiple matches is a content-build error listed in `BUILD-ERRORS.md` and on `/methods`. Do not re-point it at a similar sentence.
- **Nothing implies approval or peer review.** The banner (§4f) is on every route and cannot be dismissed. `manifest.venue` is rendered on `/` and `/about`. `as_of` (2026-10-03) appears on `/`, `/read`, `/about`, both maps and `/systems`.
- **No runtime fetches.** No tiles, no geometry, no fonts, no analytics, nothing fetched at runtime. Map geometry is bundled at build from the `us-atlas` npm package (§4d). Fonts are self-hosted.
- **Fail loudly in the content build.** Schema violation, unresolved `[n]`, unknown term/concept/section/block id, missing data file, a map layer with no `layers.json` entry or a `layers.json` entry with no column, a FIPS code with no geometry, a ledger source number with no reference, a change or open item pointing at an unknown block → non-zero exit with a clear list. Write `content-pack/BUILD-ERRORS.md` and continue with whatever validated.
- **Delivery mode is `pages`**, GitHub account **`feelingsarereal`** (§6). `gh auth status` must show that account as active, or you stop. Never push this app to any other account.
- Vault contract: touch nothing outside this folder except K7's `state`; `notes/`, `source/` and the 🚀 hub note are excluded from the repo via `.git/info/exclude`.

## 4b. The reader's source layer — normative

`content-pack/claims-ledger.yaml` is a list of 2,123 entries, one per claim the report makes:

`id` (block id + letter, e.g. `B0133.b`; `n1`, `n2` for claims introduced during revision) · `block` · `section` · `kind` (figure | event | status | description | role | absence | analysis | self) · `status` (verified | corrected | updated | reattributed | newly-sourced | analysis | self | absence | removed | unverified) · `quote` (the words in the block that state the claim) · `anchor_ok` · `original_claim` (the claim as the 3 October draft made it, restated; **for corrected and updated entries this is the superseded claim, so it may be shown only on `/changes` and in the claim card under the heading "As the draft had it"**, never as the current claim) · `sources[]` = `{n, quote, locator}` (the supporting passage from reference `n`, at most 25 words, and where it sits) · `looked` (for `absence` and `unverified`: where it was looked for) · `note` · `checked`.

**Anchoring.** Match `quote` inside the block with that id, after normalising both sides: drop `**`, `_` and `*` emphasis markers and HTML comments, treat `<br>` as a space, collapse whitespace. A quote containing ` | ` is a list of fragments from one table row, each of which must be found in that table (anchor the first fragment's cell, or the row). Quotes may contain `[n]` tokens exactly as the block does. `anchor_ok: false` entries (2) and `removed` entries (30) are not anchored; list the former on `/methods`.

**What the reader sees.**

- **Citation hover.** Every `[n]` in the reader is a button. On hover, on keyboard focus, and on tap, it opens a popover (Floating UI; Esc closes; focus returns):
  1. the reference: title, publisher, date, and a chip for how it was read ("read in full" / "read in part");
  2. **the reference's `summary`**, in full;
  3. under the heading **"What supports this sentence"**: for each ledger entry in the same block whose `sources` include `n`, the claim's `quote` (trimmed to its first 18 words) → the source's `quote` in quotation marks with its `locator`; at most three shown, then "and k more in this block". If no entry in the block cites `n`, omit the heading;
  4. links: "Open the source ↗" (new tab), "Full reference →" (`/references#ref-n`).
  The popover must be reachable and readable at 375 px (render as a bottom sheet there). Clicking `[n]` still opens the fold-out card under the paragraph (APP-SPEC §5), which shows the same content in full, without the three-item limit.
- **Claim status, on demand.** A reader-level toggle in the reader's toolbar, **"Show what was checked"**, off by default and persisted. When on, every anchored claim gets a quiet underline in a colour for its status group: *verified* (verified); *changed* (corrected, updated); *re-sourced* (reattributed, newly-sourced); *the report's own* (analysis, self); *not found* (absence); *unsourced* (unverified). Use `manifest.palette.groups.verified / changed / open` and neutral tones; never red/green alone (shape or pattern plus colour), and include a legend. Hovering or focusing an underlined claim opens the **claim card**: status in words ("Corrected on 3 October 2026"), the supporting passages with their references, `looked` for absences ("Looked for in: …"), "As the draft had it" for corrected and updated entries, and a link to the change entries for that block.
- **Change markers.** A block with one or more entries in `changes.yaml` gets a small marginal marker (a dot with the count; "changed" as its accessible name). Activating it opens the block's changes inline: a word-level diff of `old` → `new`, the `reason`, the reference chips, the type and weight. A filter in the toolbar, "Substantive changes only", limits the markers.
- **Section header counts.** Each top-level section (the eight domains, the four sections, the guide, the appendices) shows a one-line strip: claims checked, changed, open items — each a link to the filtered `/changes` or `/open-items`.
- **Deep links.** `/read#b-B0133` scrolls to and briefly highlights a block; `/read#<section-id>` works as usual. Every claim card, change and open item links this way.
- The dismissible banner at the top of the first section reads: "Written by Little Orange Fish. Every claim was checked against its cited source on 3 October 2026; hover a bracketed number for the source and the passage that supports the sentence. Passages marked *analysis* are the report's own reading."

## 4e. Systems graph — normative

`figures/data/systems-graph.json`: `nodes[]` = `{id (d1…d8, s9…s12), label, title, kind (domain | cross-sector), number, section, scope, admh_plan_goals}`; `edges[]` = `{id, from, to, row, pair, link, evidence, refs, block}` — 17 edges from the 13 rows of the report's dependency table (a row with two targets is two edges sharing a `row`); `reading` — the report's closing paragraph on the map. `systems/indicators.json`: `headline[]` = `{label, alabama, united_states, rank_of_51, note, refs, nodes, block}` (56 rows from the report's baseline tables, each assigned to one or two nodes); `all_figures_by_node` = for each node, every sourced figure claim in that section of the report (`claim`, `text`, `status`, `refs`, `section`, `block`); `map_layers_by_node`.

- `/systems` renders the twelve nodes in a **fixed, deterministic layout**: the eight domains on a ring in numeric order, the four cross-sector sections on an outer arc beside the domains they most connect to. No force simulation. Edges are directed arrows; an edge's label is its `link`. Domains and cross-sector nodes are distinguished by shape as well as colour.
- **Select a node:** a panel with its title, its `scope` (the report's own scope paragraph, with live citations), the ADMH plan goals the report says it touches (labelled "goal numbering is Little Orange Fish's own"), its incoming and outgoing links, its **headline indicators** (Alabama, United States, rank of 51 where given, note, reference chips), "All sourced figures in this part of the report (n)" as an expandable list where each row links to its block in `/read`, and "On the maps" links from `map_layers_by_node`. A link "Read this section →".
- **Select an edge:** the `link`, the `evidence` paragraph with live citations and term links, and a link to the dependency table in `/read`. Selecting an edge highlights its two nodes and any sibling edge from the same row.
- A mode switch **"Depends on" / "Is depended on by"** highlights, for the selected node, everything upstream or downstream in the drawn edges (computed by walking the edges; label it "following the report's thirteen links").
